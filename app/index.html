How much of this instrument is actually working

This is a research prototype. It was built to test whether a disciplined, evidence-based process can track scientific ideas over time — not to pick investments. Before reading any result below, here is exactly how far it got.

  • The 13-thought cohort is a provisional pilot. It is one small subfield, drawn from one literature query. It is not a sample of anything wider, and no conclusion here generalises beyond it.
  • Only the first of five gates was ever evaluated. Gates 2 to 5 were never reached, so nothing here speaks to whether these ideas could be built, commercialised or financed.
  • Six of the eight matrices cannot be computed. Too little evidence exists at the level of an individual scientific proposition to score them. That is a declared gap, not a finding about the science.
  • The split you see rests on a single dimension. Of 38 designed dimensions, one — STM‑2, whether a paper supports its mechanism with its own data — decides which thoughts advance. The other scored dimensions are identical across all 13. Treat the advance/monitor split as that one judgement, restated.
  • We tested the settings, not the science. Varying every weight and every coverage threshold changes no outcome. That shows the result is not an artefact of our chosen numbers. It does not show the result is correct.
  • No independent expert has checked this yet. Every judgement was made by one reader. A blinded review package is prepared and unused. Until two independent readers agree, the reliability of the key dimension is unmeasured.
  • There is no recommendation here, and no probability. No score is a likelihood of success, financing or return. The matrices measure how much documented evidence exists, and nothing more. They are never combined into a single number.
