What this is, and what it is not

This is a research prototype, not a product, and it did not work. Its central hypothesis was tested against a pre-registered protocol and the result was negative: the method performed worse than simply ranking papers by citation count. That result is published here rather than buried, because the point of the exercise was to find out.

Origin Engine asks a narrow question: can a deterministic, evidence-based process identify scientific ideas early, follow them over time, and reconstruct how they contribute to real technologies — using only information that existed at the time?

It makes no investment recommendation, produces no probability of success, and has no demonstrated predictive performance. Nothing on this site should be read as investment advice or as a claim that the method can identify opportunities early.

Purpose

Why build it at all

Deep-tech investing depends on reading science before it is obvious. The question is whether that reading can be made systematic and auditable rather than tacit. Origin Engine was built to test that, on one small, historically closed subfield — pig-to-human kidney xenotransplantation — with a hard cutoff of 30 September 2021. Everything the system sees was publicly available before that date. Everything after it is used only to check the answers.

Architecture

How it is put together

Evidence ledger

Append-only, checksummed, cutoff-enforced. Four distinct date types; event dates are never inferred from publication dates.

Cohort

13 scientific propositions extracted from 18 eligible sources under a published merge rule, from a frozen literature query.

Assessment

Eight matrices, 38 dimensions, five sequential gates. Scores measure documented evidence against fixed anchors — never likelihood.

Missing vs negative

A score of zero and “no evidence found” are different claims and are never conflated. A failed search is never recorded as scientific absence.

Versioning

Every methodology change is a version bump with the prior version frozen under checksum. Parameters live in configuration, not code.

No composite

The eight matrices are never summed into a single number, by rule — a blended score would invite exactly the predictive reading the project disclaims.

Capabilities

What it demonstrably does

  • Reproducibility. Identical inputs produce identical outputs, byte for byte, across processes and sessions.
  • Temporal discipline. The historical cutoff is enforced programmatically and verified by test, not asserted.
  • Refusal under pressure. It declined four pieces of available evidence that would have flattered it — including a 2024 paper that directly corroborates its own selected case, 28 months outside the observation window.
  • Separating what is unknown from what is absent. Of 494 dimension records, 362 are explicitly marked as missing evidence with a reason, rather than scored as zero.
  • Self-correction. Five confident conclusions were overturned during development by running the measurement rather than reasoning about it. Each correction is in the repository with its evidence.

The experiment

The result was negative, and that is the finding

A pre-registered experiment asked whether the method could recover the documented scientific ancestry of the first pig-organ transplants in humans, using only pre-cutoff evidence. The protocol, the evaluation set and all candidate rankings were frozen under SHA-256 before any answer key was retrieved.

Origin Engine protocol16.0%40.0%fails all three decision tests
Plain date-ordered retrieval20.0%46.0%beats Origin Engine
Pre-cutoff citation ranking32.0%54.0%best method tested

The diagnosis mattered more than the score. Of 317 documented antecedents available before the cutoff, only 50 were in the search corpus at all. Of the 267 missing, just six predated the corpus window — 261 sat inside it and were missed by the topical query. Sampling those, only 10% mention xenotransplantation: they are single-cell methods, assay platforms, renal physiology and general immunology.

The ancestry of a breakthrough is mostly not in its own field. A subfield-bounded corpus structurally cannot contain it, so the corpus boundary — not the scoring — was the binding constraint. No amount of ranking fixes that.

Limitations

Stated plainly

  • No independent review has occurred. Every judgement was made by one reader. A blinded review package is prepared and unused.
  • Effective dimensionality is one. Of 38 designed dimensions, 12 were ever scored, nine of those are identical across all 13 propositions, and a single dimension determines every result.
  • Six of eight matrices cannot be computed — too little evidence exists at the level of an individual proposition.
  • Only the first of five gates was ever evaluated.
  • No outcome variable. Nothing in the cohort has a recorded success or failure, so no predictive claim is testable on it.
  • One subfield, one cutoff, no replication. Nothing here generalises beyond pig-organ xenotransplantation.

Transferable method

What actually transfers to an investment workflow

The matrices did not survive testing. The development discipline did — and that is the part worth reusing. Every item below describes the approach used to build and falsify this prototype, not a validated capability.

Deal sourcing

A frozen query, explicit eligibility rules and an append-only evidence ledger make a sourcing thesis auditable: what was seen, when, and why something was excluded. This prototype also shows the failure mode — a topical query missed 82% of what mattered.

Investment diligence

Separating “no evidence found” from “evidence of absence”, and recording a search status for every open question, turns a diligence memo into a list of resolvable gaps rather than a verdict.

Portfolio monitoring

Append-only reassessment with a hard as-of date shows what changed between two points in time and — more usefully — proves when nothing changed, without rewriting the earlier view.

Finance

Deterministic, checksummed, versioned outputs: identical inputs reproduce identical figures, and every parameter change is a recorded version bump with the prior version preserved.

Investor relations

Reporting that states its own limits. This site leads with a negative result and a seven-point disclosure — the same pattern applied to performance reporting builds more trust than selective presentation.

The general pattern

Pre-register the question, freeze the method under checksum, define the failure condition in advance, then publish whichever answer arrives. It is cheap, and it is what made this prototype's negative result trustworthy.
